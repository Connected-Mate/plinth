## Design Context

### Users
Owners of a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor…) who also carry an iPhone. Mostly curious makers, not necessarily developers. They land here from a link, want to understand in 5 seconds what Plinth does, then copy one prompt.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, exact, confident. "Ultra empty, ultra designed." Emotional goal: calm + a small "oh, nice" on every touch. Three words: bare, heavy, precise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clone of the app home screen: light grey #F2F2F2 canvas, fine dashed square grid with dots at intersections, huge black condensed uppercase display (Plinth Display = Archivo Black, OFL), system sans body, ONE white pill card max per view, round black buttons, single accent #FF4D1A used only for focus rings / one live dot. Light + dark (tokens from the app's design.css). Anti-references: SaaS landing pages, feature-card grids, gradients, Inter, emoji.

### Design Principles
1. One idea per screen; whitespace is the material.
2. Show, don't describe: the hero IS the app's home screen and its button works.
3. Every control answers (press scale, copied toast, live dot) — transform/opacity only, out-quint, reduced-motion safe.
4. Tokens come from the app (`plinth/skill/plinth-design/design.css`), never invented.
5. Honest: no fake screenshots, no invented numbers; "coming soon" when it is.
